Cruise Travel Tips

📄
Passports Required
Valid passport required — must be valid 6 months beyond completion of travel with enough blank pages for entry endorsements. Names must match booking exactly.
🛳️
Princess MedallionClass
The Medallion device enables keyless stateroom entry, cashless payments, and on-demand service. Download the Princess app before boarding to set up your account.
💱
Onboard Currency
Ship's currency is US Dollars. Princess MedallionClass allows seamless cashless payments. Credit cards accepted for onboard accounts.
🚬
Smoking Policy
Smoking is not permitted in staterooms, balconies, or most indoor areas. Designated outdoor smoking areas are available on deck.
🦽
Mobility
Wheelchair access is available at all ports. Contact Princess or Tokui Travel in advance for special requirements. Shore excursions vary in mobility level.
🌦️
Weather in May
British Isles in May: 10–18°C. Pack layers — sunny one moment, rainy the next. Bring a light rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes for port days.
⚕️
Health
No vaccinations required for the UK and Ireland. Pack any prescription medications in carry-on luggage with doctor's note. The ship's medical centre is available 24/7.
📱
Communications
Princess has MedallionNet WiFi packages available. For port days, local SIM cards work in the UK and Ireland. Disable roaming on return to avoid charges.
